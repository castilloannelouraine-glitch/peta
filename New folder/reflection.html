My Garment Reflection

When choosing and wearing appropriate garments,
I learned that it is important to ensure that the clothing is suitable for the event or activity that I will be attending.

Garments affect my comfort and confidence through making me feel presentable, while providing warmth the I am seeking.
In addition, if the garment chosen is not appropriate, it can cause distress or you will feel uncomfortable and think that your outfit is not suitable.
