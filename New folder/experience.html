My Experience Wearing Garments

I feel comfortable and confident at the same time when wearing different garments.
Since every garment should be worn accordingly, I wear warm clothes when I'm feeling cold. On the other hand, I wear clothes that feel cool when I am experiencing warm weather.
To summarize, different types of garments has its own uses. But personally, I prefer my garments to be comfortable and loose so that I could move freely.
