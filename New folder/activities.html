Garments and Activities

  • School – School Academic uniform
  • Sports – Physical Education uniform
  • Special occasions – Blouses, Skirts, Dresses
  • Everyday activities – Shirts, Shorts, Pants

These garments are appropriate for these activities because it satisfies the certain needs of that task.

In school, it is a must of wear the School Academic Uniform for normal days,
and PE uniform for the days that we need to do sports because it can help us move more freely.
It is important to do so because it is prescribed by the school administration to promote orderliness and unity.

For special occasions, I can wear whatever I want to as long as it follows the theme.
I wear semi-formal garments for these as it is commonly suitable.

Lastly, for everyday activities, I wear something that is comfortable such as shirts
because this is just a common day.
